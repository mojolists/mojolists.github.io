The Mission

For the Crate Diggers.

MojoLists was born out of a simple, obsessive need: to document the records that actually matter. In an era of algorithmic playlists and fleeting digital singles, we believe in the sanctity of the Album.

We aren't here to give you a "best of" list generated by a bot. We are here to talk about the midtempo grinds, the whiskey-soaked croons, and the radioactive hamster bites of musical genius that define a lifetime of listening.

Whether it's the retro-soul of The James Hunter Six or the deep cuts of the Blue Note era, our archive is a living document of analog love in a digital world.
