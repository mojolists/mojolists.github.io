It's Only Us

The Monophonics

Colemine Records • 2020

"It feels like the soundtrack to a lost 1970s film set in the Bay Area."

It's Only Us is a deep dive into the psychedelic soul sound that The Monophonics have spent years perfecting. From the opening title track, the production—led by Kelly Finnigan—is lush, fuzzy, and cinematic. It feels like the soundtrack to a lost 1970s film set in the Bay Area.

What sets this record apart is the songwriting. While the grooves are impeccable, the lyrics touch on reflection and connection in a way that feels modern despite the vintage gear used to record it. The horns are warm, the backing vocals are haunting, and Finnigan’s soulful delivery ties the whole project together. It’s a standout release for Colemine Records and a must-listen for anyone into the "Sagan soul" movement.
