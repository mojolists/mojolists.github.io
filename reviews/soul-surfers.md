Hailing from Nizhny Novgorod, Russia, The Soul Surfers managed to capture a sound that many American bands spend decades chasing. 

<div class="mojo-quote">
    "Soul Rock! is exactly what the title promises—a heavy, distorted blend of raw funk and psychedelic rock that feels like it was unearthed from a 1971 time capsule."
</div>

The production on this record is gloriously "lo-fi" in the best way possible. It’s crunchy, the drums hit with a dry thud, and the organ leads slice through the mix with a biting grit. It’s mostly instrumental, letting the groove do the talking, and it proves that soul music is a universal language.
