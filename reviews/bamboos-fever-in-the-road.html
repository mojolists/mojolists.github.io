Australian Funk Feature

The Bamboos

Fever In The Road (2013)

The Bamboos have always gotten a lot of love from me and I expect a lot when they have a new release. Fever In The Road hits on all cylinders and for those new to The Bamboos, they are an Australian funk/soul band that has miraculously managed to do more than simply survive, they have thrived.

Mastermind, bandleader and multi-instrumentalist Lance Ferguson has maintained a musical vision that many have attempted but few pull off. The ingredients are your standard funk fueled horn section, a power rhythm section and honey-dipped female vocalists Ella Thompson and Kylie Auldist.

I am pleased to say The Bamboos are heavy supporters of vinyl and I happily gave the double LP a spin. The small price to step up to the vinyl was well worth it. The recording is spacious with instruments and vocal placement well defined in the soundstage. Backing instruments and percussion swirl around the melody playfully on the LP.

If it isn’t clear, I have a major crush on this band. Fever In The Road is currently in heavy rotation whenever I need a funky dance bop to get me through the day. Do yourself a favor and grab this as soon as possible, I promise it will make you feel better no matter what ails you.
