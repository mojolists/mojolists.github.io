Over the last few years, I slowly stopped writing and reviewing. I let the site go down and kept my thoughts to myself. I couldn’t get over the hump of why it mattered.

Neal Francis’s new album “Changes” touched me, it was a hand reaching down into the murky depths of my malaise. 

<div class="mojo-quote">
    "Neal Francis has serious keyboard skills and a rare gift for crafting earworm hooks. He has resurrected a classic sound for a new era."
</div>

For Neal, this album is his own road back, from a life left behind for the dark sweetness of addiction and all the humbling horror it creates. 

<div class="btn-container">
    <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hf4kC90iUao" target="_blank" class="action-btn">Watch Video</a>
</div>

Focusing his New Orleans gained superpowers to create flourishes of Dr. John, Allen Toussaint and Leon Russell, Neal creates a solid funk and soul album.

</div>

Give “Changes” a spin and see if it doesn’t help you resurrect an old passion. Just make sure you have that New Orleans funk as the soundtrack for whatever you do next.
