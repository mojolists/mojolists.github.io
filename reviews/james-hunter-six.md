James Hunter has been a favorite of mine since I first heard *People Gonna Talk* years ago. He has a way of channeling the 50s and 60s R&B sound that feels completely authentic, never like a parody. With his latest Daptone release, *Nick Of Time*, he and his long-standing band have managed to strip things back even further, resulting in an album that is lean, punchy, and incredibly soulful.

The title track "Nick Of Time" sets the tone immediately with its mid-tempo groove and Hunter's signature vocal rasp. What I love about this record is the production; it’s warm and immediate, sounding exactly like a band playing together in a room. Daptone’s commitment to analog recording shines through here—there’s a depth to the saxophone and a snap to the drums that you just don't get with modern digital sessions. It’s a short, sweet record that demands to be played on vinyl.
