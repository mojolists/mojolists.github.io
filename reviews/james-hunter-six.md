James Hunter has been a favorite of mine since I first heard *People Gonna Talk* years ago. He has a way of channeling the 50s and 60s R&B sound that feels completely authentic, never like a parody. 

<div class="mojo-quote">
    "Daptone’s commitment to analog recording shines through here—there’s a depth to the saxophone and a snap to the drums that you just don't get with modern digital sessions."
</div>

The title track "Nick Of Time" sets the tone immediately with its mid-tempo groove and Hunter's signature vocal rasp. What I love about this record is the production; it’s warm and immediate, sounding exactly like a band playing together in a room. 

<div class="btn-container">
    <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T8GRSU2t73o" target="_blank" class="action-btn">Watch Video</a>
</div>

It’s a short, sweet record that demands to be played on vinyl. The songwriting is tight and the band is perfectly in sync.
