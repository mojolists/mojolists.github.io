Album Review

Say That To Say This

Trombone Shorty

Released: 2013 // Verve Forecast

Trombone Shorty (Troy Andrews) doesn't just play jazz; he plays "Supafunkrock." On *Say That To Say This*, he captures the sweltering, crowded energy of a New Orleans street parade and bottles it into a studio masterpiece.

"It’s the sound of a musician who knows exactly where he comes from, but isn't afraid to see where the funk can take him."

Working with producer Raphael Saadiq, Shorty finds a balance between technical virtuosity and pure, unadulterated groove.
