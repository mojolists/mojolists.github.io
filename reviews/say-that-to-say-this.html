Vinyl Review // Jazz-Funk

Say That to Say This

Trombone Shorty’s masterclass in New Orleans production.

There’s a specific kind of pressure that comes with being the face of New Orleans music. On Say That to Say This, Troy "Trombone Shorty" Andrews doesn't just meet that pressure—he distills it into a 10-track run of surgical funk.

The Pressing

We tested the 180g black vinyl pressing. The first thing you notice is the separation. In a genre that often gets "muddy" in the low-mids, the production here keeps the brass sharp and the pocket deep. The title track, in particular, showcases a snare snap that will reveal exactly how fast your woofers really are.

Sonic Texture

This isn't a "lo-fi" jazz record. It is polished, aggressive, and incredibly wide. Andrews’ trombone has a grit that feels physical through a good tube stage, vibrating the air with a weight that digital masters often smooth over.
