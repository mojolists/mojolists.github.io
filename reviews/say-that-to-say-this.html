Vinyl Review // Jazz-Funk

Say That To Say This

Trombone Shorty

Fig 1. Original 180g Verve Pressing

There is a specific kind of pressure that comes with being the face of New Orleans music. On Say That to Say This, Troy "Trombone Shorty" Andrews doesn't just meet that pressure—he distills it into a 10-track run of surgical funk.

The Recording

This isn't a "lo-fi" jazz record. It is polished, aggressive, and incredibly wide. Andrews’ trombone has a grit that feels physical through a good tube stage, vibrating the air with a weight that digital masters often smooth over.
