Review Entry #001

Changes

Neal Francis

The Pressing

180g Black Vinyl

Label

Karma Chief

Reference Gear

Technics SL-1200MK7
Ortofon 2M Blue
Yamaha A-S801

Key Tracks

1. Changes
2. She's a Winner
3. Problems

Neal Francis creates a solid funk and soul album that is as danceable as it is thought provoking. From the moment the needle drops on the title track, you are transported to a world where the Wurlitzer is king and the groove is the only law.

The recording quality on *Changes* is remarkably consistent. Unlike many modern "throwback" records that lean too heavily on artificial lo-fi effects, Francis and his team at Colemine Records have captured a genuine sense of space and analog saturation. The drums snap with a dry, 1970s studio crispness, while the bass sits deep and round in the mix.

"A masterclass in analog warmth that proves the soul of the sound is alive and well in the 21st century."

On the B-side, the record slows down just enough to let the songwriting breathe. Tracks like "Problems" showcase a vulnerability that balances out the high-energy funk of the opening. For the audiophile, this pressing is impressively quiet, allowing the subtle nuances of the keyboard textures to shine through during the quieter passages.

Mojo Verdict

Essential

Score: 9.2 / 10
