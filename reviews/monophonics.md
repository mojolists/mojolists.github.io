*It's Only Us* is a deep dive into the psychedelic soul sound that The Monophonics have spent years perfecting. From the opening title track, the production—led by Kelly Finnigan—is lush, fuzzy, and cinematic. 

<div class="mojo-quote">
    "It’s a standout release for Colemine Records and a must-listen for anyone into the 'Sagan soul' movement."
</div>

What sets this record apart is the songwriting. While the grooves are impeccable, the lyrics touch on reflection and connection in a way that feels modern despite the vintage gear used to record it.

The horns are warm, the backing vocals are haunting, and Finnigan’s soulful delivery ties the whole project together. It’s a standout release for Colemine Records and a must-listen for anyone into the "Sagan soul" movement.
