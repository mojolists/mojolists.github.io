Clarence "Gatemouth" Brown was never just a bluesman, and *San Antonio Ballbuster* is the definitive proof of his "American Music" philosophy. This collection captures Gatemouth at his most ferocious, blending Texas swing, big band jazz, and stinging electric blues into a sound that no one else could replicate.

<div class="mojo-quote">
    "Whether he’s picking up the fiddle or tearing through a guitar solo, there is a sense of sophisticated swing that keeps the music moving."
</div>

His guitar work here is legendary—sharp, percussive, and incredibly fast. But it’s the way he leads the band that truly impresses. The tracks are lean, mean, and perfectly represent the post-war Texas blues sound.
