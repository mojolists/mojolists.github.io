Trombone Shorty

Say That To Say This (2013)

The story of Troy ‘Trombone Shorty’ Andrews career started at the age of four, when he and his chosen instrument stood on stage with Bo Diddly. The crowd and Mr. Diddley were blown away all those years ago and Trombone Shorty has never looked back.

Now Say That To Say This is Shorty’s third album for the Verve label and is yet another step forward for this ever pushing musician.

My favorite track, Fire and Brimstone, is a steady rock jam with backing horns and grinding rhythm. Each song pushing you down the road effortlessly.
