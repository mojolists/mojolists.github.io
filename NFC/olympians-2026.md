The second full-length from The Olympians, a 17-piece NYC instrumental soul ensemble led by keyboardist Toby Pazner. Their self-titled 2016 debut was a cult favorite built around a Greco-Roman mythology concept; *In Search of a Revival* arrived ten years later on Daptone with a very different frame — cinematic, noir, post-pandemic. Twelve tracks, no vocals, all mood.

During the decade between albums, Pazner was touring as the keyboardist for Lee Fields, and you can hear that road time in the arrangements. The core players are drawn from El Michels Affair and the Menahan Street Band, so the pedigree is about as deep-Daptone as it gets.

**Cool stuff:**
- Recorded **live to tape at Daptone's Penrose studio in Riverside, CA** — the same room that produced Thee Sacred Souls and The Altons
- Tracks move through distinct film-genre moods: **"Hollywood Cold"** is noir slow-burn, **"California"** channels Wrecking Crew mid-century cool, **"Saraswati Puja"** pulls in Indian strings for spy-movie exotica, **"Honey Bea"** closes with late-Beatles warmth
- Zero overdubs on the rhythm section — the band tracked the grooves together in a room and let the tape breathe
- Part of the **Daptone x Colemine collaboration** series — rare cross-label release between two of the biggest soul revival labels currently operating

**Spin it for:** widescreen soundtrack vibes, late-night driving music, zero-vocal instrumental soul that plays like a lost '70s film score.

**Standouts:** "California" · "Strawberry Kiwi" · "Saraswati Puja" · "Hollywood Cold"
