The third studio album from the London jazz quintet — drummer/bandleader Femi Koleoso, bassist TJ Koleoso, keyboardist Joe Armon-Jones, trumpeter Ife Ogunjobi, and saxophonist James Mollison — released on Partisan Records in September 2024. It's the followup to *Where I'm Meant to Be* (2022), the record that won them the Mercury Prize and made them the first jazz act ever to do so.

The whole album is built around the concept of a night out dancing, sequenced as a full arc from the cloakroom to the last call. Short interstitial "Act" tracks thread the thing together like chapters. Guests include Olivia Dean, Yazmin Lacey, M.anifest, and Moonchild Sanelly, but the core quintet is front and center throughout — this is not a features record.

**Cool stuff:**
- Recorded live-in-studio at **Abbey Road** — the full band playing in a room, not stitched together in isolation booths
- Debuted at **#7 on the UK Albums Chart** — Ezra Collective's first-ever Top 10, and a rare feat for a jazz record in a pop chart
- Named **#33 on Mojo's Best Albums of 2024**
- Femi Koleoso also drums for **Gorillaz** and **Jorja Smith** — his pocket on this record is straight from the London music scene he came up in, not the jazz conservatory

**Spin it for:** deep grooves, punchy horns, danceable jazz that rewards both headphones and a full room.

**Standouts:** "God Gave Me Feet For Dancing" (Yazmin Lacey) · "Ajala" · "No One's Watching Me" (Olivia Dean) · "Everybody"
